# Summoner's Los Santos

**gta5** × League of Legends · regle · idea by the team

League of Legends takes over Los Santos: the player gets champion-style Q W E R abilities with cooldowns, and minion waves march down the streets toward a glowing nexus downtown. Destroy the enemy nexus to win.

> I dropped a League of Legends lane into GTA V: my Starfall meteors flattened the enemy turret, then the nexus blew up in slow motion.

## Install

Copy the mod folder into your FiveM server's resources as sigf_mod, add ensure sigf_mod to server.cfg and join. A lane with two nexus towers appears where you stand; minion waves march. Press Q, Z, X and C to cast Q, W, E and R (rebind them in FiveM key settings).
